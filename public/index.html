VeriDeck

Verify a startup pitch against real data.

VeriDeck pulls the company and founder claims out of a pitch deck (headcount, funding, founding year, founders’ past jobs, hiring) and checks each one against Crustdata. Every verdict comes with a reason and a source.

Or paste your pitch One click: extracts claims, finds the company, verifies everything. --- between slides is optional.
